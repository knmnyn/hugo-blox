Tim Baldwin is a Professor of Natural Language Processing at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) and a Melbourne Laureate Professor at The University of Melbourne. He will join WING as a Sabbatical Visiting Professor from November 2026.

His research spans natural language processing, deep learning, algorithmic fairness, computational social science, and social media analytics. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from Tokyo Institute of Technology and served as President of the Association for Computational Linguistics in 2022.
